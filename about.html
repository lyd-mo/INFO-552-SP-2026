The Junk Journal Collective

About Us

The Junk Journal Collective is a community of journalists, crafters, creators, and artists who share a passion and curiosity for creating junk journals. We believe in the beauty of repurposing and upcycling materials to create unique and meaningful art pieces.

Our collective provides a supportive space for members to learn, share, and connect with others who have a love for junk journaling.

Whether you're a seasoned junk journaler or just starting out, we welcome you to join our community. We offer resources, tutorials, and inspiration to help you on your creative journey. Our members come from all walks of life, but we are united by our love for junk journaling and our desire to create something beautiful out of the ordinary.

Join us in celebrating creativity, sustainability, and the joy of making with what we have. Together, we can inspire each other and create a vibrant community of junk journal enthusiasts!
