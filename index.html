The Junk Journal Collective

Welcome!

Welcome to the Junk Journal Collective! This is a community of creators who make junk journals and share our work online. Our mission is to inspire creativity and connect junk journalers from around the world.

Whether you're a seasoned junk journaler or just starting out, we invite you to explore our website, join our community, and share your own creations with us!
